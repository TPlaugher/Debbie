About Debbie

Debbie is a CPA with almost twenty years of experience in accounting and system integration. She began her career in audit at Deloitte, then moved into industry as Controller for an oil and gas services company. There she led a small accounting team, produced the monthly financials, and managed the selection and implementation of Sage Intacct.

That project turned into a career. Debbie has been a Sage Intacct consultant for more than ten years, building solutions for clients in not-for-profit, software, and professional services. She knows both sides of the work: what a controller needs at close, and how to configure the system to deliver it.
